# EuroSys'27 Paper Strengthening

## Goal
Use the remaining technical-content allowance to make the bounded semantic
reducer unmistakably a runtime/operator contribution, without changing frozen
evidence or broadening claims beyond the two same-family checkpoints and
label-conditioned external replay.

Current extension: replace the weak impression of a self-only Figure 3 with a
fair, same-task comparison against the strongest reproducible non-LLM reducer
that can be implemented without labels, while keeping the budget sweep as an
ablation rather than mislabeling it as SOTA.

## Seven-step argument
1. Problem: model text can mutate analysis state without an execution contract.
2. Importance: data runtimes need replayable, rejectable semantic transitions.
3. Gap: data operators, workflow tools, and structured output each leave part
   of evidence eligibility, commit, rollback, checkpoint, or trace ownership
   unspecified.
4. Idea: a bounded `Edit` proposal interpreted by system-owned `Validate` over
   an evidence-linked baseline.
5. Feasibility: integrate at reducer boundaries without replacing storage,
   partitioning, scheduling, or model serving.
6. Evaluation: controlled workload obligations, fault matrix, two same-family
   online scales, conditional cost, and narrowly scoped public replay.
7. Takeaway: model judgments can be runtime-managed state transitions rather
   than generated workflow state.

## Constraints
- Do not rerun or rewrite frozen raw online evidence.
- Primary result remains nine families, three seeds, five samples at 7B.
- 14B is a same-family scale check, not cross-family robustness.
- AIOpsArena is label-conditioned reducer-only replay, not end-to-end detection.
- Technical content must end by page 12; references may follow.
- No NPU or online rerun is authorized for this extension; new baseline rows
  are `simulation/model` runs over repo-local controlled workloads, and only
  their summaries/figures are `derived-artifact`.
- The baseline may use only evidence/candidate fields available to all reducers,
  never injected incident labels, hidden scorer state, or replay-only episode IDs.

## Strong-baseline protocol

- Method: deterministic constrained agglomerative evidence clustering.
- Observable inputs: evidence score, region, time interval, service dependency,
  signal overlap, and optional upstream hint.
- Hard exclusions: different regions and contradictory non-null root hints.
- Output: common evidence-linked hypothesis schema; root hint may be inserted
  into affected services exactly as in the existing hybrid baseline.
- Leakage test: output must be byte-equivalent after every
  `source_incident_id` is removed or replaced.
- Evaluation: all nine families and ten fixed seeds under the existing matrix
  runner/scorer; report aggregate and per-family results even if the new method
  matches or exceeds the model-backed path.
